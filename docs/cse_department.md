## 1. Department Overview

The Department of Computer Science and Engineering at JNTUH UCEST was established in **1984**. Recognized as one of the preeminent computer science departments in India, it offers undergraduate (B.Tech), postgraduate (M.Tech), and doctoral (Ph.D.) degrees.

- **NBA Accreditation**: Tier-1 (Valid through 2027)
- **Sanctioned Undergraduate Intake**: 60 students per batch (+10% Lateral Entry)
- **Student-to-Faculty Ratio**: 14:1
- **Average Placement Rate**: 92% across eligible graduates

---

## 2. Vision and Mission

### Vision
To be a leader in computing education and research, fostering creative, globally competitive computing professionals committed to solving society’s grand challenges.

### Mission
1. Provide deep theoretical foundations paired with intensive hands-on programming and system engineering.
2. Advance state-of-the-art research in artificial intelligence, cloud architectures, data analytics, and cyber security.
3. Foster interdisciplinary innovation in partnership with premier industry leaders and global universities.
4. Nurture professional ethics, open-source contribution, and leadership mindsets.

## 5. Laboratories & Technical Infrastructure

### Lab 1: Artificial Intelligence & Deep Learning Lab
- **Hardware**: High-end workstations with NVIDIA RTX 4090 GPUs, 64GB RAM.
- **Software**: PyTorch, TensorFlow, CUDA Toolkit, OpenCV, JupyterHub.
- **Utilization**: Machine Learning, Deep Learning, NLP projects.

### Lab 2: Cloud Computing & Distributed Systems Lab
- **Hardware**: Enterprise rack servers, 10Gbps dedicated optical backbone.
- **Software**: OpenStack, Kubernetes, Docker, Apache Spark, Hadoop clusters.
- **Utilization**: Cloud virtualization, big data analytics, microservices architectures.

### Lab 3: Database & Web Technologies Lab
- **Hardware**: 65 modern Dell OptiPlex core i7 systems.
- **Software**: MySQL 8, PostgreSQL, Oracle 19c, React, Node.js, Spring Boot.
- **Utilization**: Full-stack web application development, DBMS query optimization.

### Lab 4: Networks & Cyber Security Lab
- **Hardware**: Cisco Enterprise routers, managed switches, firewall appliances.
- **Software**: Wireshark, GNS3, Kali Linux, Snort IDS, Metasploit.
- **Utilization**: Network protocol analysis, ethical hacking, cryptographic benchmarking.

### Lab 5: Systems Programming & Algorithms Lab
- **Hardware**: 60 high-performance Linux workstations (Ubuntu LTS).
- **Software**: GCC, Clang, GDB, Valgrind, Python, OpenMP.
- **Utilization**: Data structures, advanced algorithms, compiler design, OS kernel tinkering.
